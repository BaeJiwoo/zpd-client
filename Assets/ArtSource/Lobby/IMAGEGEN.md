## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production Unity 2D UI sprite atlas, not a mockup. Create a single transparent-background 1536x1024 PNG sprite sheet arranged in exactly THREE EQUAL COLUMNS and TWO EQUAL ROWS, six isolated sprites, one centered in each 512x512 cell with generous clear margins and NO overlap. Style: bold, playful flat vector cartoon, very thick near-black ink outlines, angular/chamfered corners, hard two-tone cel-shading, matching a game character with giant black eyes, spiky violet hair and simple chunky black silhouettes. NO gradients, NO texture, NO words, NO letters, NO numbers, NO characters or scenery. Top-left cell: large almost-square empty pale-lavender UI panel with a thick black frame, subtle violet lower bevel, broad completely plain fill for text. Top-middle cell: large almost-square empty warm ivory item-card panel with thick black frame and warm-gray lower bevel. Top-right cell: wide horizontal blank violet button, about 420x180 px, thick black outline, simple lavender upper face, darker purple bottom extrusion. Bottom-left cell: wide horizontal blank golden-orange battle button about 420x180 px, thick black outline, pale gold upper face and burnt orange bottom extrusion. Bottom-middle cell: chunky simple violet backpack icon, thick black outline, one light lilac front pocket and two gold buckles, no surrounding panel. Bottom-right cell: social/friends icon of two simple warm cream cartoon head-and-shoulder silhouettes, thick black outlines, violet back silhouette, no facial details, no surrounding panel. Panel and button middle regions must be perfectly flat blank solid colors for Unity nine-slicing; keep all bevel details close to the edges. All six sprites entirely visible with transparent gutters and real alpha transparency, no checkerboard background, no drop shadows outside the black outline.
